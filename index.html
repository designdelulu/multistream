Watch Twitch, Kick, YouTube & TikTok LIVE streams online

MultiStream.cc is a modern multi-stream viewer for Twitch, Kick, YouTube, and TikTok LIVE (experimental). Add channels from the toolbar and share a Live Watch Party — one link where viewers automatically follow what you're watching as you change streams.

Streams are arranged in a responsive grid — landscape players scale to the largest 16:9 size that fits, and portrait streams (YouTube Shorts, TikTok LIVE) keep their true 9:16 shape in dedicated 2-row slots. Static path URLs look like /t:username/k:username/y:handle:username/tt:creator; live watch parties use /w/ROOM_ID.

Welcome to MultiStream.cc

Watch Twitch, Kick, YouTube, and TikTok LIVE (Experimental) side by side in one responsive grid — a modern multi-stream viewer with minimal friction. Add channels, rearrange the grid, then share a Live Watch Party so friends follow what you're watching automatically.

Quick start

  • Type a username and pick Twitch, Kick, YouTube, or TikTok LIVE (Experimental) from the suggestions — or paste a full stream URL and the provider is detected automatically.
  • Open Share to start a Live Watch Party and copy a live link at /w/ROOM_ID. When you add, remove, replace, or reorder streams, everyone in the party follows along.
  • For TikTok LIVE, the suggestion accepts a username, @username, or a full live/share URL.
  • Use Focus on a stream to expand it and turn sound on.
  • Switch to Focus view for one large primary stream and a tray of the rest — click a tray stream's header to swap it into the primary spot without interrupting playback.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: 1–9 pick a stream, F toggles the Focus tray, M mutes the big stream, Esc returns to the grid.
  • Hover the toolbar icons to Share, Clear, hide headers, or open Twitch or Kick chat.
  • Prefer a denser grid? Hide headers — at rest you see video only; hover a card and a strip opens below it (focus, remove, drag).

On a phone, streams stack in a column. About three fit on screen at once. Scroll to move between them. Twitch may need you to stay on it a moment before the video fully plays.
