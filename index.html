Watch Twitch and Kick streams online

MultiStream.cc lets you watch Twitch and Kick streams online on a single page — as many as your computer can comfortably handle. Add channels from the toolbar, or open a link with your lineup already in the URL.

MultiStream arranges players in a responsive grid and scales each stream as large as possible while keeping a clean 16:9 aspect ratio. On phones and tablets, streams stack vertically so you can scroll through your lineup one player at a time.

On phones and tablets you can keep several streams in the page, but the browser usually plays only one at a time due to platform restrictions.

Share a layout with a link

Add streams to the URL path, separated by slashes. Use t: for Twitch and k: for Kick:

multistream.cc/t:username/t:username/t:username/k:username/k:username/k:username
