Watch Twitch and Kick streams online

MultiStream.cc is a free multi-stream viewer for Twitch and Kick. Add channels from the toolbar or open a shareable URL with your lineup already configured.

Streams are arranged in a responsive grid that scales each player as large as possible while keeping a clean 16:9 aspect ratio. Share path URLs like /t:username/k:username with your watch party.

Welcome to MultiStream.cc

Watch Twitch and Kick side by side in one responsive grid. Add channels from the toolbar, rearrange them however you like, and share your lineup with friends.

Quick start

  • Type a username and pick Twitch or Kick from the dropdown.
  • Use Focus on a stream to expand it and turn sound on.
  • Hover the toolbar icons to Share, Clear, hide headers, or open Twitch chat.
  • Prefer a denser grid? Hide headers — a left Watching list appears so you can still focus or remove streams.

On phones and tablets you can keep several streams in the page, but the browser usually plays only one at a time due to platform restrictions.
